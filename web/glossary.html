GLOSSARY

Plain-English definitions for every term used on Macro Beans. No jargon, no maths, no Sharpe ratios.

TERMS

alphabetical
AT CLOSE
Entering or exiting the trade at the price the market closes at for the day. You can see the day's full move before you decide.
AT OPEN
Entering or exiting the trade at the next trading day's opening price. You decide overnight, before any new news has had a chance to land in the price.
CUMULATIVE RETURN
The total gain or loss from when you entered the trade up to a given day. A "+1.5%" on Day 3 means you would be up 1.5% three days after entering — not 1.5% on each of those three days.
DOWN DAY
A day where the closing price was lower than the previous day's close. Used by strategies that look for crashes to buy the bounce.
ENTRY
The moment you start the trade — when you buy (if going long) or sell (if going short).
EVENT
A historical day where the strategy's setup actually happened. The event log shows every one of them in the data we have.
HOLDING PERIOD
How long you stay in the trade after entering. Macro Beans shows Day 1, Day 2 and Day 3 so you can see how the trade evolves over the short term.
INSTRUMENT
What you're trading — for example the S&P 500 stock index, gold, Brent crude oil, or the FTSE 100.
MEAN REVERSION
The idea that prices that move very far in one direction tend to come back toward their average. "Buy the Bounce" is a mean-reversion strategy: it bets that a hard drop will partly reverse.
MEDIAN
The middle value when you line all the returns up smallest to largest. Less affected by one or two big outlier trades than the average is — often a better picture of the "typical" trade.
MOVE THRESHOLD
How big the day's move has to be to count as an event. A −2% threshold on a down day means "only show me days that fell by 2% or more".
RETURN
The percentage change in price between two points in time. "+3%" means up 3%, "−1.5%" means down 1.5%.
SAMPLE SIZE
How many past events fed into the numbers on a page. Bigger is more trustworthy. Anything under about 10 events is too small to lean on.
TRIGGER MOVE
The actual size of the move on the event day — the thing that got that row into the table. If your threshold is −2%, the trigger move might be −3.4%.
UP DAY
A day where the closing price was higher than the previous day's close. Used by "fade the rally" style strategies that bet a big spike will partly reverse.
WIN RATE
The percentage of past trades that finished in profit. A 60% win rate means 60 out of 100 past examples made money. Doesn't tell you the size of the winners or losers — read it alongside the average and worst-trade numbers.
WINS
How many of the historical trades ended in profit, out of the total number of events.
WORST TRADE
The single biggest loss across all the historical events. The risk you're really taking on if today turns out to be one of those days.
