ABOUT MACRO BEANS

A free playground for retail traders who want to see whether an idea has actually worked in the past — before they put real money on it.

WHAT IT IS

Each page on Macro Beans takes one trading strategy — like "Buy the Bounce" — and shows you every time that setup has happened in the past, what came next over the following few days, and a short, plain-English summary of the result.

The goal is to make the kind of analysis that quants do every day accessible to anyone who's curious about why a strategy might (or might not) work. No jargon, no maths beyond a win rate and an average.

HOW IT WORKS

You pick an instrument and a few simple settings — direction, move size, when to enter, how far back to look. The page pulls the full historical price record for that instrument, finds every day that matched your settings, and shows you what happened on each of the three days after.

All of the filtering and counting happens live in your browser. There's no signup, no account, no chatbot, and no tracking. The price data is refreshed once a day after the US market closes.

WHAT IT'S NOT

Macro Beans is not a brokerage, a trading platform, a tip service, or financial advice. The numbers on every page describe what happened in the past. Past results are not a promise of future returns — markets change, and a setup that worked 70% of the time over the last five years can still lose on the very next trade.

If you're new to trading, treat every page here as "is this idea even worth thinking about?" rather than "is this a buy signal?". Always size your trades so that the worst-case loss is one you can comfortably afford.
