SUMMARY

WHAT IT IS

Stock markets don't move evenly through the year. Habits like year-end buying, summer lulls and tax deadlines leave a faint calendar footprint. This page takes a market's whole history, works out the return for each calendar month, then averages each month across all the years on record.

A word of caution: seasonality is one of the weakest patterns in markets. Ten or fifteen years is a small sample — a single crash in one September can colour the whole month. Treat a strong-looking month as a mild lean, never a guarantee. Months built on only a handful of years are flagged below.
