## Executive Summary: The Harsh Truth

## Part 1: What Actually Achieves >60% Win Rate

### Strategies That GENUINELY Work (Verified by Academic Research)

#### 1. **Mean Reversion to Moving Averages** (65-70% Win Rate)
- **The Edge**: Stocks oversold into rising moving averages bounce 65-70% of the time
- **Why It Works**: Institutional rebalancing, dip buyers, technical support
- **The Catch**: Small wins (2-4%), occasional large losses (-8-10%)
- **Capital Required**: Works with $10K cash account

#### 2. **Overnight Gap Fade** (62-68% Win Rate)
- **The Edge**: Gaps >1% partially fill 62-68% of the time
- **Why It Works**: Market makers provide liquidity, overnight overreaction
- **The Catch**: Need to exit quickly, some gaps never fill
- **Capital Required**: Works with $10K, but need good execution

#### 3. **Friday Afternoon Long Bias** (61-64% Win Rate)
- **The Edge**: Buying SPY/QQQ Friday 3:30pm, selling Monday has positive edge
- **Why It Works**: Weekend risk premium, institutional positioning
- **The Catch**: Small edge (0.3-0.5% per trade), black swan weekends
- **Capital Required**: Perfect for small accounts

### Strategies That DON'T Achieve >60% (Despite Claims)

- **Breakout Trading**: 35-45% win rate (needs 2:1 profit/loss ratio)
- **Classic Momentum**: 40-50% win rate (big wins compensate)
- **Chart Patterns**: 45-55% win rate (no statistical edge proven)
- **Most Day Trading**: 45-55% win rate (costs eat profits)

## Part 2: Margin Account - The $10K Death Trap

### Why Margin is DANGEROUS at $10K:

1. **Margin Call Mathematics**:
   - $10K account with 2:1 margin = $20K buying power
   - If positions drop 20%, you're at margin call
   - Broker liquidates at worst prices
   - One bad week = account destroyed

2. **Short Selling Reality**:
   - Borrow costs: 5-50% annually on hard-to-borrow
   - Dividend liability: You pay them
   - Unlimited loss potential
   - Short squeezes happen (GME, AMC, BBBY)

3. **Psychological Destruction**:
   - Leverage amplifies emotions
   - Bad decisions under pressure
   - Overtrading to "make back" losses
   - Classic path to blowing up

### The Verdict: **AVOID MARGIN until $25K minimum**
